How the five-year assumptions work

All 21 benchmarks retain their published forecasts uncapped. An annual EPS series can inform a uniform-accrual NTM timing model. Explicit calendar/NTM definitions are distinguished from assumed calendar alignment or an assumed NTM horizon. The model requires six calendar growth inputs to produce five forward-anchor transitions; uncovered calendar years use the sourced long-term EPS proxy. A new calendar estimate can affect two adjacent NTM transitions because those earnings windows overlap.

Published consensus estimates and house scenarios keep their original calendar, publisher-year or forecast-date labels. When a house forecast’s measurement interval is unspecified, the default uses the sourced long-term EPS proxy throughout. Annual publisher labels may be modeled as calendar years and an unspecified forward denominator may be modeled as NTM, with those assumptions visible. These are not matched publisher NTM forecasts. All holding-year rates remain editable.

Long-term nominal EPS projections combine Invesco’s real earnings growth, inflation and buyback/dilution building blocks. The workbench names the market or regional proxy, source date and calculation for each benchmark. Buybacks enter EPS once; dividends and the selected exit P/E are calculated separately. All five rates remain editable, and custom yearly assumptions are protected.
