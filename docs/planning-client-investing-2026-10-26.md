# Client-facing investing guides

Four English articles (with Simplified Chinese editions) dated 26 October 2026, written for clients rather than advisors:

- `risk-profile-loss-limit-time-horizon-stress-test` (`riskProfileStress`): risk tolerance versus risk capacity, stress-tested on generic 20–100% stock mixes; the profile is the lower of the two answers, as described in Joint CSA/CIRO Staff Notice 31-368 (10 December 2025).
- `fund-facts-risk-rating-standard-deviation-yearly-range` (`fundRiskBand`): the NI 81-102 Appendix F bands (0–6, 6–11, 11–16, 16–20, 20+) turned into bell-curve yearly ranges in dollars.
- `mutual-fund-series-advice-included-fee-based-cost-comparison` (`fundSeriesCost`): MER with trailer versus fee-based MER plus a separate advice fee and sales tax; break-even advice fee.
- `non-registered-fund-switch-capital-gains-tax-fee-saving-break-even` (`fundSwitchTax`): after-tax liquidation comparison of keeping a fund versus switching to a cheaper one.

All scenarios are hypothetical. No firm model portfolios, suitability limits, internal compliance material or client information is used. Defaults and every number quoted in the text are reproduced in `tests/planning-client-investing.test.mjs`.
