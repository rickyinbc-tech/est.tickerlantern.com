# Waiting for a dip and rebalancing through a crash

Two English articles dated 26 October 2026, each with four editable figures and chart data tables:

- `waiting-for-a-dip-new-contributions-cash-rule` (`dipWait`): new monthly pay held in cash until the index is a set percentage below its running peak, or until a waiting limit, compared with investing each deposit at month-end. Distinct from the existing lump-sum staging, exit/re-entry and DCA cash-floor guides.
- `balanced-fund-rebalancing-crash-drift-equity-share` (`rebalanceDrift`): a target-mix portfolio rebalanced within a band versus the same portfolio left to drift through a crash, a recovery, a second decline and a no-crash variant. Explains that a balanced fund rebalances internally and that sales in a self-managed non-registered account can be dispositions.

Dated official observations: Statistics Canada all-items CPI +3.1% year over year, September 2026 (released 19 October 2026); Bank of Canada target for the overnight rate 2.25% on 22 October 2026 (Valet series V39079). Other sources: FINRA DCA (19 May 2026), FINRA market timing (10 June 2025), FINRA asset allocation and diversification, CRA TFSA (page dated 17 September 2026), CRA RRSP, CRA T4037 Capital Gains – 2025. No US tax rules are applied to Canada and no personal tax amount is calculated.

Dip model order at each month-end: index return, interest on cash already waiting (effective annual rate converted to monthly), new deposit, then a decision on the observed close. All waiting cash is invested when the drawdown from the running peak is at least the trigger or the oldest waiting deposit has waited the limit. A zero limit reproduces investing every deposit. The three 36-month index paths are authored teaching examples, not history or likelihoods. Defaults (CAD 500/month, early-dip path, 10% trigger, 12-month limit, 2.25% cash): regular CAD 21,127.278379; waiting rule CAD 20,759.792018. Steady rise: 19,214.114563 vs 19,002.819558 (trigger never fires). Late dip: 18,603.230194 vs 18,826.164664.

Rebalancing model: annual bucket returns, then a year-end trade to target when equity deviates by more than the band (0 = every year). No contributions, withdrawals, fees, spreads or tax. Defaults (CAD 100,000, 60% equity, −35%/+2% year one, three 15% recovery years, 6% later, 3% bonds, 5 pp band, 10 years): rebalanced CAD 142,505.136601; drift CAD 137,372.965839. Second-decline variant: 123,307.962098 vs 118,719.681230, with troughs 72,378.60 vs 74,199.00. No-crash variant: 160,715.330002 vs 161,207.516966.

All defaults were reproduced independently with 40-digit Python Decimal arithmetic. Tests cover the zero-limit identity, an independent dip ledger, flat/zero cases, observed-close causality, trade value conservation, equal-return and 0%/100% equity identities, band-zero targeting, field bounds, related-link resolution and four finite figure/table pairs per guide.
