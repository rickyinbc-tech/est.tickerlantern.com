# Billable-hour capacity and annual cash allocation

One detailed English article adds four figures and data tables. All rates,
hours, costs and targets are authored hypothetical inputs. No personal or
client record is used.

## Evidence and method

CRA's RC4110 public HTML (Rev.23) and BDC's pricing overview (updated
1 August 2023) were read on 25 October 2026. Original public HTML copies,
retrieval dates and SHA-256 hashes remain ignored and owner-restricted under
qa/billable-capacity-sources. CRA supports the distinction between work facts
and an elected label. BDC supports general cost/overhead/break-even context.
No legal classification test, current statutory contribution or market-rate
statistic is embedded.

A 52-week equal-week template removes non-overlapping leave and downtime.
Available hours split into billable and unpaid working hours; no-work capacity
is separately labelled. All billable capacity is assumed sold and collected
within the year. There is no collection timing or demand response.

Collected fees exclude sales tax. Variable costs are a fee percentage. Fixed
business cash costs and a separately entered personal provision leave cash
before personal tax, CPP/QPP, EI and other personal liabilities. The provision
is counted once, with no deemed deduction or benefit equivalence. The remaining
amount includes compensation for owner work and business risk; no owner wage
is separately deducted. This cash bridge is not formal accounting profit.

The required rate solves the stated annual pre-personal-tax target exactly.
No billable capacity reports no available billable hours, with fixed costs and
provision retained. A numeric solved rate is not a commercially attainable
quote or a salary comparison.
