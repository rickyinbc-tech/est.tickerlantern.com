How the five-year growth estimates work

Keep each exact benchmark’s published future rates. Fill missing years by a linear fade to a rounded nominal EPS anchor at year five. Before fading, cap the final published rate’s excess or shortfall relative to the anchor at 10 percentage points. This avoids carrying a temporary earnings rebound through all five years. Current-year estimates are context only. Custom edits affect only the selected year.

Anchors range from 4.5% for mature UK/Japan markets to 9% for India, with 6% for the S&P 500 and 8% for Nasdaq-100. These are TickerLantern judgement assumptions, not sourced five-year consensus. They do not impose a sustainable-growth funding test; the sensitivity matrix shows alternatives. Retained or failed source checks remain visible above.
